How to Grow Tomatoes at Home

Category: Vegetables

Tomatoes are one of the easiest vegetables to grow. They need lots of sun, water every two days and a stick to hold them up as they grow.

  1. Choose a sunny spot.
  2. Plant the seedling deep in the soil.
  3. Water at the bottom, not on the leaves.
