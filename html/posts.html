How to Grow Tomatoes at Home 🍅

Tomatoes are one of the easiest vegetables to grow! Here are my 3 simple tips:

  1. Choose a sunny spot.
  2. Plant the seedling deep in the soil.
  3. Water at the bottom, not on the leaves.
